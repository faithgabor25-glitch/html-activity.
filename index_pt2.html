Watching You Return to Someone Else

Written by: Kheryne Faith Gabor

There was a time when i believed that staying was always better than leaving.
I thought that as long as two people loved each other, they could overcome anything.
But eventually, I realized that love can become exhausting when you are constantly trying to hold on,
to something that is slowly draining you.

I reached the point where I became tired.
I was emotionally exhausted, and I knew that i needed to choose myself.
So, I decided to end our relationship. It was not an easy decision.
Even though I was the one who said goodbye, I still cared about her,
and a part of me still hoped that things could somehow become better.

After our break up, we continue talking. She would tell me that she missed me,
and that she wanted us to fix things. Those words made everything more confusing because,
despite everything that happened, I still had feelings for her.

Then i found out that she was also trying to fix things with her previous girlfriend,
the person she had been with before me. Eventually, they got back together,
and now they are living together in the same roof.

When she told me, I did not know how to react. I felt a kind of pain that was difficult to explain.
It felt like my heart was breaking all over again. What made it harder was knowing that,
she could still message me and say that she missed me even though she had already chosen,
to be with someone else.

I kept asking myself why it hurt so much when i was the one who decided to leave.
I realized that ending a relationship does not automatically mean that your feelings dissapear.
Sometimes, you can know that leaving is the right decision and still miss the person you left behind.

Maybe I was not meant to stay in her life forever. Maybe our relationship was meant to teach something instead.
It thought me that loving someone does not mean forgetting yourself.
It thought me that being tired is already a reason to stop,
and that choosing your peace is not selfish.

It still hurt when i think about what happened. I still miss the person she used to be in my life.
But I am slowly learning that missing someone does not mean i have to return to them.
But sometimes, i was left wondering. Did she miss me?, Did she miss us?,
I hate that a part of me still cares. Sometimes, I wonder if I was the one who got away.

Maybe someday, I will hear her name and feel nothing but peace.
Maybe someday, I will stop wondering why she chose someone else.
Maybe someday, I'll stop wondering why you went back to her.
Maybe someday, I won't care who you're with.
Maybe someday, hearing that you miss me won't make my heart question whether i should come back.

But today isn't that day. Today, It still hurt. Today, I still miss you.
And today, I'm still learning how to accept that someone can mean the world to you,
and still not be meant to stay. So i'll let myself grieve what we were.
I'll let myself miss you without chasing you.
I'll let myself hurt without destroying myself.

Sometimes, Letting go is not about stopping yourself from loving someone.
It is about finally learning to love yourself enough to walk away.
And maybe, someday, I will look back at this story and realize that losing her was not the end of my story.
It was simply the beginning of finding myself again.

Note from the Author:

I didn't write this because I want her back. I wrote this because sometimes,
even when you know leaving was the right choice, your heart still mourns what could have been.
Maybe I wasn't mean to keep her. Maybe I was meant to find myself after losing her.
And maybe this pain is simply the price of finally choosing me.
